5. THE CONSUMERS OF AN OUTLINE VERTEX -- AND THE ONE THAT MISBEHAVES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
By AST, not grep: 55 reads of `.outline` and 5 writes, across 6 files and 20
functions --

  rooms.py     36 reads / 5 writes  _derive, area_sqft, path, corners,
                                    bind_room_walls, open_edges,
                                    open_edge_segments, room_walls,
                                    rooms_holding, share_outline_vertices,
                                    repair_edge_bindings, export_properties,
                                    split_partially_covered_edges,
                                    walls_cover_room, coalesce_outline_corners,
                                    _translate
  extract.py    6   extract_room, join_room
  walls.py      5   _plan_vertex_moves, collapse_degenerate_outline_edges
  mainwindow.py 4   _claim_region_walls, _translate_shape
  items.py      3   _corner_records, bake
  bridge.py     1   apply_design_to_scene

MOST ARE INDIFFERENT. `path`, `area_sqft`, `_derive` and `corners` read the
vertex's POSITION, and a divorced vertex is at the right position -- which is
why every area in every measurement above is unchanged to the cent, and why
the state is invisible to a user until something moves.

THE ONE THAT MISBEHAVES IS `walls._plan_vertex_moves`, the drag gather, at
walls.py:1979: `dv = by_id.get(id(e.v))` -- BY IDENTITY. A divorced edge is not
found and is left where it is while its wall moves.

AND THE REPO ALREADY KNEW. `close_gap` (walls.py:1061-1119) calls
share_coincident_ends and then repairs every room on the floor, with this
comment:

    RESTORE THE P3.5 INVARIANT: the fold above re-binds WALL ends onto one
    anchor vertex per corner, and an outline left holding a
    coincident-but-distinct twin is a stranding the NEXT drag turns into a
    diagonal tear (found at the P4.2 mini-gate: close the gaps, drag a wall,
    and M Bath / Hall / Lounge drew dashed diagonals to corners their walls no
    longer held).

That is D62, named, with its symptom, found by a manual check at P4.2 -- so the
harm is established by precedent in this repository, not only by my one
ambiguous sample.

THE MECHANISM, EXACTLY: `share_coincident_ends` has three callers.

    close_gap        walls.py:1101   -> repairs EVERY room on the floor
    join_room        extract.py:238  -> repairs the JOINED room only (:239)
    weld_scene       walls.py:556    -> DOES NOT REPAIR

The repair already exists, is named `share_outline_vertices`, is idempotent,
and its necessity is already documented at the one site that does it. The two
gaps are weld_scene (all of it) and join_room (the neighbours it does not
touch, which is what the walk's step 3 caught).
