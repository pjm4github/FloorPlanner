WHAT THE MEASUREMENT TURNED UP THAT WAS NOT ASKED FOR
-----------------------------------------------------
Validating the orphan count split it in two, and the second half is the larger
finding:

  TRULY ORPHANED  no wall end lies at this outline corner at all.
                  wiscaway 7 -> 29 across normalize_walls.
  DIVORCED        a wall end IS here, and holds a DIFFERENT Vertex object than
                  the outline does. Under the Phase 3 model -- "a corner is one
                  Vertex the walls and the outlines both hold" -- this state
                  should not exist.
                  wiscaway 0 -> 49, planc1 0 -> 56, farmplace 0 -> 24,
                  rounded 0 -> 78, symmetricP1 0 -> 57.

  ZERO ON EVERY PLAN AS LOADED; LARGE ON EVERY PLAN AFTER Edit > Coalesce all
  walls now. Attributed to weld_scene on all five. The mechanism is visible in
  the counts: weld folds coincident wall ends onto one surviving Vertex and
  does not rebind the room outlines holding the loser.

  THE SHIPPED INSTRUMENT CANNOT SEE IT. scene_identity_report (D48/G2) reports
  extra_vertices 0 on four of the five plans after normalize_walls, and 6 on
  planc1 -- because it compares WALL ENDS TO WALL ENDS. An outline corner
  holding a vertex no wall holds is outside the question it asks. Its zero is
  not evidence that the scene's corners are shared.

  THE CONSEQUENCE IS NOT ESTABLISHED, AND IS NOT CLAIMED. WallItem gathers the
  outline edges a corner drag must carry with by_id.get(id(e.v)) at
  walls.py:1979 -- by identity -- so a divorced edge is skipped. Read that way
  it is stranding. Measured, ONE sampled drag on a divorced corner (Lounge,
  (456, 768)) moved the wall, left that corner where it was, and the room's
  area did not change: consistent BOTH with stranding and with the designed
  step-insertion at walls.py:1999-2014. One sample does not separate them.
  Filed as D62 with the consequence open.

-----------------------

its predicate identified: the LOOSE per-room count above. Cross-checked against

    Dining     11 corners of which 7   MEASURED 11 / 7

saying so.
