THE FOUR BLANK-CANVAS AFFORDANCES -- is there a route outside that path?

Measured 2026-08-08 by walking the live menu bar (every QAction and its
shortcut) and by parsing every call site of the underlying method.  Parse for
existence, run for reachability -- the two questions are asked separately, and
by different instruments, for the reason D53 already paid for.

  affordance          alternative route                          verdict

-- PASTE ROOM: WHY Edit > Paste IS NOT IT -----------------------------------

There are TWO clipboards and they are not connected:

  item_clipboard   written by cut_selected / copy_selected
                   read by paste_clipboard  <- Edit > Paste, Ctrl+V
  room_clipboard   written by rooms.py:1270 (the ROOM menu's "Copy room")
                   read by mainwindow.py:1582 (paste_room) ONLY

paste_room's only caller is view.py's blank-canvas Room-tool menu, gated on
`a_paste.setEnabled(self.win.room_clipboard is not None)`.  So Ctrl+V does NOT
paste a room, and "Copy room" has exactly one partner.

  AND THE PAIR IS SPLIT ACROSS THE TWO MENUS: "Copy room" is on the ROOM's own
  context menu -- which A1b has just made reachable from the whole region --
  while its "Paste room" partner is on the BLANK-CANVAS menu.  Copy is now
  easier to reach than it was and paste is harder.

-- 3D VIEW: TWO CALL SITES, BOTH BLANK-CANVAS -------------------------------

  levels.py:239   the floor popup's appended "3D view..." -- and ONLY when
                  scene_menu=True, i.e. the RIGHT-CLICK route.  The keyboard
                  route (Ctrl+F) deliberately excludes it; select_floor_popup's
                  own docstring says so: "a chord named 'select a floor' should
                  not offer a renderer".
  view.py:674     the Room-tool blank-canvas menu.

No menu-bar entry.  No shortcut.  No toolbar button.  THE 3D VIEWER IS
REACHABLE ONLY BY RIGHT-CLICKING EMPTY CANVAS -- and on a plan that fills the
canvas there may be no empty canvas to right-click.

  This was ALREADY fragile before A1b: it always required blank canvas.  What
  A1b removed is the region route -- on main a right-click inside a room
  returned None, so the room's own area counted as "empty canvas" and offered
  it.  The viewer was reachable through a hit-testing defect, which is the same
  shape as the naming affordance and the fourth instance of that pattern.

-- WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE CONTEXT-MENU PASS ----------------------------------

Two of the four are covered and the pass need only preserve them.  Two are
gaps, and closing them is part of the pass rather than a follow-up:

  * 3D view needs a first-class route.  A menu-bar entry is the obvious one --
    it is a whole-document view, not a per-item action, so it belongs beside
    the other document-level commands rather than on any context menu.
  * Paste room needs to be reachable where Copy room is, or to share Ctrl+V's
    key with a modifier.  Where exactly is a design call: paste wants a
    LOCATION, which is why it sat on the canvas menu in the first place.
