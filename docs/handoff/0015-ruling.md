# 0015 — ruling: the four furniture generators are RETIRED

**Patrick's, 2026‑08‑14.** Quoted rather than summarised.

---

## The ruling

> **RETIRE THE FOUR GENERATORS — FORMALLY, AS RULED BY ME. `seat`, `bed`,
> `basin` and `enclosure` generators are not deferred, not pending, RETIRED.
> Region extrusion covers what they were wanted for, from artwork already on
> disk, with one generator that generalises rather than four that each know one
> kind.**

**They were never written.** Nothing is deleted by this ruling; what closes is
the *expectation* that they would be, which had been live since
[`0010-ruling.md`](0010-ruling.md)'s item TWO — *"the remaining generators in
descending item count"*.

**`vehicle` is NOT retired with them.** Its case is the **loft** design in
[`../../floorplanner/viewer/VIEWER_NOTES.md`](../../floorplanner/viewer/VIEWER_NOTES.md)
§5 — a car-shaped slab against a car — which still stands; only its urgency
changed.

## On leaving the ruling to be made rather than inferred

> **You were right to leave the ruling to me rather than infer it — "not needed"
> is a measurement and "retired" is a decision, and the register should not blur
> them.**

Recorded because the distinction is reusable: **a measurement can make a
decision obvious without making it.** The register holds both kinds of entry and
they read alike on the page, so the line between them is kept by whoever writes
the entry — and an inferred ruling is indistinguishable from a taken one a month
later.

---

## THE REUSABLE PART — the sequence, not the outcome

> **Record the sequence that produced it as the reusable part: build the cheap
> general mechanism, re-measure, then decide whether the specific ones are still
> wanted. Four functions unwritten is the receipt.**

> ### BUILD THE CHEAP GENERAL MECHANISM, RE-MEASURE, THEN DECIDE WHETHER THE SPECIFIC ONES ARE STILL WANTED.

**What it replaced.** The original plan was to build five form generators in
descending item count — `vehicle` 10, `enclosure` 7, `seat` 6, `bed` 4, `basin`
1 — with the stopping rule *"stop when the remainder is not worth a function"*.
That asks for a judgement **in advance**, on counts, about work nobody has seen
the results of.

**What actually happened, in three steps:**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **the cheap general mechanism** | `prism` — extrude the plan symbol. No new authoring; the data already existed for every item |
| 2 | **RE-MEASURE** | which items still fell back, and what the survivors actually looked like |
| 3 | **DECIDE** | region extrusion covered the rest; the four were never wanted |

**THE RECEIPT IS FOUR FUNCTIONS UNWRITTEN.** Not four functions deleted, not
four deprecated — never written, and the plan that called for them closed
without them.

**Why the order matters, and it is the part that generalises:** building the
specific ones first *guarantees* work the general mechanism would have made
redundant, and you cannot know how much until the general one exists. The
sequence costs one extra measurement and can save an arbitrary amount of
building.

**Two conditions it depends on**, stated so it is not applied where it does not
fit:

* **The general mechanism must be genuinely cheap** — prism consumed data that
  already existed and needed no new authoring. A general mechanism that costs
  more than the specific ones is not this pattern.
* **The re-measurement must be real, and its receipt fixed in advance.** *"Build
  it and see"* is not this — the measure was ruled before the work started
  (*"prism's receipt is a re-measurement, not a claim"*), which is why the
  number could not be replaced by an impression afterwards.

**And it needed one correction along the way**, kept because it is part of the
pattern rather than a blemish on it: the first re-measurement said **28 → 1**,
and that overstated the win. The honest number was **the outer outline is a
plain rectangle for 17 of 18** — prism had given the furniture a box by a
different route. **The re-measurement step is only as good as what it measures**,
and here it took Patrick looking at a real plan to find that the sofa was still
a slab.
