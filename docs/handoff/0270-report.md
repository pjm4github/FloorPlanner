## 1. WHAT A LEADER IS

A note with an arrow: the **N** tool presses on the thing to name — the
point snaps as a measure's end does (the datum, a corner, an opening's
jamb, a wall's centreline, else the grid) — drags to where the text goes
(the grid), and on the release asks for the text. A cancelled dialog
leaves nothing; a click without a drag puts the text 24″ up and to the
right of the point. Drawn in the annotations' ink: the line from the
point to the text's end, a filled arrowhead at the point (nine view
pixels at any zoom), a short horizontal shoulder from the line's end on
the side away from the point, the text on the shoulder (twelve view
pixels). Drag the body to move the whole; select it for two small black
knobs — the arrow's point (drag with the tool's snap; Shift frees) and
the text's end (drag free); double-click edits the text; Delete removes
it; Esc mid-drag cancels. It is an annotation: Show ▸ Annotations hides
and shows it, and switching that off hands the tool back.

## 2. WHERE IT LIVES

* `annotations.py`: `LeaderItem` (`tip`, `anchor`, `text`;
  `set_points`, `move_tip`, `move_anchor`, `move_by`) and `LeaderKnob`
  (the measure's knob re-pointed: kinds `tip` and `text`); the measure's
  snap lifted to `_tool_snap(item, sp, free)` for both. `TOOL_LEADER`
  (`config.py`); the view's press/drag/release with `_temp_leader`,
  cancelled by Esc; the window's hint, toolbar action (N, the `leader`
  icon generated by `_gen_assets.py`), delete, and the Show coupling.
* **The document**: the schema's reserved `leader` kind gets its item —
  `pos` the arrow's tip, `p2` the text's end, `text` — written by the
  bridge's `_annotations_of` and read by `item_from_record`. No new
  property; the schema's description says what the kind is.
* **The PDF plan set** ([`0268`](0268-report.md)'s rule): a leader draws
  while Show ▸ Annotations is on — the line, a filled arrowhead, the
  shoulder, the text — and the sheet's frame takes its two ends in, so a
  text placed far off the walls still lands on the page.
* README: the feature paragraph and the keys row (N).
* **The macro recorder** (his words mid-build: *"I want you to add those
  tools to the V2 macro recorder"*): the annotation tools join both
  recorders and the player -- the letters `T` (Annotation), `L`
  (Measure) and `N` (Leader) in the tool table (`TOOL text|measure|
  leader` by name), and three application commands carrying what a
  dialog supplies, the `@ROOM`/`@DORMER` pattern: `@TEXT x y "text"`,
  `@LEADER x1 y1 x2 y2 "text"` (the arrow's tip, the text's end) and
  `@DATUM x y` (the Show-menu tool has no letter; its click is the
  command). The view's hooks (`on_text`, `on_leader`, `on_datum`) write
  them in either format; the raw click or drag under those tools is
  not recorded twice. A measure records as its raw `L` drag, as it
  needs no dialog. `MACRO_SPEC.md` sec14.2 and `macro_language.md`
  gain the rows; `examples.macro` a line each.
