# 0019 — ruling: the status table is generated, or it lies

**Patrick's request, 2026‑08‑15:** continue the `V5_MIGRATION_PLAN.md` Status
table past P4.5, keep the check marks current, decide who owns the update, and
amend the agreement.

**Granted in substance and refused in mechanism**, for the reason in §1.

---

## 1. THE TABLE DOES NOT STOP AT P4.5 — IT STOPS BEING TRUE

It already carries six forward rows. **One of them is lying right now:**

| row | the table says | the record says |
|---|---|---|
| **P5.2** Landscape wall types + gates | **☐** | **COMPLETE 2026‑08‑13**, PRs #26 and #27, D73 and D74 closed, Patrick's check passed |
| **P6.1** `QUndoStack` + commands | ☐ | **PARKED 2026‑08‑12** — merged and dormant, not pending |
| **P6.2** Retire snapshot undo | ☐ | **PARKED** — not pending |

**So the ask is not "extend it". A row went stale two days after it shipped**,
and adding rows to a table with that property produces more of them.

> **THIS IS THE THIRD INSTANCE OF ONE DISEASE IN THIS REPOSITORY.** The snapshot
> went **eight commits** stale carrying a bolded warning about staleness in its
> own §1. `defects/INDEX.md` was fixed by **generation plus `--check`**. This
> table is the third, and it is the one nobody was watching **because ticking a
> box feels like recording rather than like authoring.**
>
> ### A STATUS BOARD IS GENERATED, OR IT LIES. THERE IS NO THIRD OPTION AND THIS PROJECT HAS NOW TESTED IT TWICE.

## 2. THE TABLE IS DOING TWO JOBS AND ONLY ONE OF THEM CAN BE KEPT CURRENT

Read the P4.5 row: it is a **full essay** — the mini-gate items, the headline
number, the carve-out on defect 11, the constants that must be pinned. That is
not a status cell. **It is an acceptance archive, and it is excellent.**

| job | what it is | what it needs |
|---|---|---|
| **acceptance archive** — the P0–P4 rows | what was accepted, on what evidence, with what carried out of the phase | to be **frozen and never edited again** |
| **status board** — the ☐ rows | what is done and what is next | to be **regenerated and gate-checked** |

**A cell cannot be both a frozen record and a live indicator.** Trying to make
it both is what produced a `☐` beside shipped work.

## 3. THE RULING

**ONE — FREEZE THE MIGRATION TABLE.** The migration **closed 2026‑08‑11**. Its
P0–P4 rows are the acceptance record of a finished thing and **do not change
again**. A one-line header says so, so the next reader does not tick something
into it.

**TWO — THE SIX FORWARD ROWS LEAVE.** P5.1–P5.3 and P6.1–P6.3 are forward status
sitting inside a closed document, which is how one of them came to lie.
**Correct them once, in the move** — P5.2 ticked with its PRs, P6.1/P6.2 marked
PARKED rather than pending — **then they go to the board.**

**THREE — THE BOARD IS `docs/STATUS.md`, GENERATED BY `tools/status_index.py`,
AND `--check` RUNS IN THE GATE BESIDE `Docs-Index`.** Not a new mechanism: the
gate already runs `tools/defects_index.py --check` at `tools/gate.py:330`, and
that is the one thing in this repository that has never gone stale. **This is
that mechanism pointed at a second file.**

**FOUR — NOBODY OWNS IT.** This is the direct answer to the question asked, and
it is the only answer with a track record here:

> ### NO PERSON AND NO AGENT OWNS A STATUS TABLE. THE GENERATOR OWNS IT AND THE GATE ENFORCES IT.
>
> Assigning it to Code makes it a step that gets skipped under pressure;
> assigning it to the reviewer makes it a relay; assigning it to Patrick makes
> him the clerk of his own project. **All three have the same failure mode: the
> table is only as fresh as the last time someone remembered.**
>
> **And the corollary is the load-bearing half — IF A FACT CANNOT BE DERIVED
> FROM THE RECORD, THE FIX IS TO MAKE THE RECORD CARRY IT, NOT TO HAND-TICK A
> BOX.** A box that can be ticked without the record supporting it is a place
> where a claim outruns its evidence, which is the thing this whole agreement
> exists to stop.

## 4. A READ-BACK COMES FIRST, AND IT HAS A NAMED TRAP

**Do not write the generator yet.** The census doctrine applies: enumerate what
exists before building on it.

**The property to enumerate from is *a completed unit of work with its gate*** —
which is the agreement's own definition of done — **not a phase number.**

> **PHASE NUMBERS ARE A CONTAINER, AND A CONTAINER CENSUS RETURNS ITS OWN
> CONTENTS.** The furnishings programme, the D72/D71 batch, prism, region
> extrusion and grid snap **have no phase ID at all** — they live in
> `progress/furnishings.md`, `progress/side-tasks.md`, the handoff mailbox and
> the register. **A board keyed to `P<n>.<m>` would render every one of them
> invisible while looking complete**, which is the exact shape of the
> spelling-, predicate- and container-shaped censuses already on the record —
> now predicted in advance rather than found afterwards.

**What the read-back owes:**

1. **What identifies a completed unit in `docs/progress/*.md` today** — measured, per file. The three logs open with one heading each, so the answer may be *nothing machine-readable*, and that is a finding, not a blocker.
2. **If nothing does: the smallest trailer that would** — one line per entry, in the shape the log already uses. **The record gains a field; the board gains a source.**
3. **How non-phase work is keyed** — by PR, by defect id, by handoff number. Whatever it is, it must cover the work that has no phase number, since that is now most of it.
4. **What `STATUS.md` and [`SESSION_SNAPSHOT.md`](../SESSION_SNAPSHOT.md) §3 each own**, so they cannot become rival queues.

**On that last point the ruling is already made**, because the pattern exists:
**`STATUS.md` is authoritative for whether a thing is DONE; the snapshot keeps
the ORDER and the WHY.** Identical to the relationship the snapshot already
declares with `defects/INDEX.md` — *"generated and authoritative over this
paragraph."*
