## 2. RULED — THE UNDER-PASS NETWORK

For each roof A, define its **continuity network**: A's own footprint
pieces where A is currently drawn, **plus every piece where A's surface
lies strictly BELOW the drawn surface** (hidden, passing under). Excluded
from the network: pieces where A would lie above the drawn surface
(phantom air), and point contacts ([`0177`](0177-ruling.md) — positive
length only).

> **A root component certifies iff it connects to the principal anchor
> component through the continuity network.** Certified components draw
> wherever they win; hidden ground conducts connectivity but never draws.

Consequences, each a receipt:

* **rf3's rake-corner component certifies** (its corridor is rf3-under-
  rf1/rf2) — the [`0177`](0177-ruling.md) §3 rake jump comes back drawn.
* **D85's corner stays blank** — its only corridor is A-above-B, not in
  the network. Blank ground is LEGAL exactly there: root ground of an

* **The L's outer-corner B scrap certifies** (B passes under A) — so the
